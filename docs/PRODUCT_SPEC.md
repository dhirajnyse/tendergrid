# Heavyster Product Spec

## Product Thesis

Heavyster is a global heavy equipment rental listing SaaS for rental companies.

Rental companies list equipment, manage availability, upload verification details, and receive direct enquiries. Heavyster does not collect rental payments in phase one.

## Primary Users

- Independent heavy equipment rental companies
- Regional rental yards
- Crane, earthmoving, access, compaction, and roadwork specialists
- Contractors searching for machines by category, location, and availability

## Phase 1 Non-Goals

- Heavyster does not collect rental payment
- Heavyster does not own equipment
- Heavyster does not guarantee delivery
- Heavyster does not provide insurance
- Heavyster does not mediate damage disputes
