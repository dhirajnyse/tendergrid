# Heavyster Brand Direction

## Positioning

Heavyster is the direct listing network for heavy equipment rental companies.

The brand should feel strong, global, practical, and trustworthy. It should not look like a generic construction marketplace or a consumer classified site. The first impression should be: verified machines, real rental companies, direct enquiry, no commission games.

## Tagline Options

- Heavy equipment. Listed direct.
- The rental listing network for heavy equipment.
- Verified heavy equipment, direct from rental companies.
- List heavy. Rent direct.
- No commission until we help close the booking.

## Visual System

- Core colors: coal black, industrial amber, verified teal, warm off-white.
- Logo: a blocky isometric H built from steel plates, with amber for equipment energy and teal for verification/trust.
- Tone: concise, operational, confident.
- Avoid: playful cartoons, generic excavator clip art, crypto-style gradients, overused marketplace badges.

## Brand Promise

Heavyster helps rental companies become discoverable online without forcing them into a transaction model before the market is ready.
